POWERCORE / GDEYOUNG

Battery power, per source. One widget.

PowerCore replaces the stock battery widget: separate power profile, screensaver, lock, sleep and keyboard backlight for plugged-in vs on battery — plus a charge-protection toggle, clamshell mode, and live watts on the bar.
Per-source power profile & idle timings
Battery protection toggle (UPower, no root)
Clamshell: keep running, lid closed
Keyboard backlight per power source
Live watts — true whole-system draw on battery
Optional battery gauge across the whole bar
Right-click cycles %, watts, or both
No daemon, no udev rule, no root
